natural language processing nlp is a branch of artificial intelligence it helps computers understand and process human language in nlp is used in many applications such as chatbots machine translation sentiment analysis and text summarization text preprocessing is an important step in nlp it includes several operations such as cleaning text removing punctuation and removing unnecessary numbers for example a chatbot may process customer messages every day these messages can contain capital letters numbers like punctuation marks and extra spaces the main goal of text preprocessing is to convert raw text into a clean and consistent format this makes the text easier to analyze and use for further natural language processing tasks
